About

My name is Kevin Helvig. I'm a researcher working on AI and computer vision for robotics at ONERA. I explore the use of non-conventional imaging techniques such as thermal imaging. My PhD thesis was about the use of infrared flying-spot laser thermography coupled with deep learning to identify defects on metallic surfaces, exploring the development of deep learning based sensor-fusion neural models through cross-attention approaches. Data augmentation techniques using denoising diffusion probabilistic models associated with curriculum learning approaches is one of my main subjects of PhD work.
